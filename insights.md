## Summary insights
This dataset includes a large volume of healthcare admissions across hospitals, insurance providers, and medical conditions.
- Total records: 55,500  
- Distinct hospitals: 39,876  
- Insurance providers: 5  
- Medical conditions: 6

## Overall billing summary
The dataset shows consistent billing patterns across patient admissions.
- Total billing across all admissions exceeds $1.4B.
- Average billing per admission is approximately $25.5K.
- Minimum and maximum billing amounts indicate a wide range of treatment complexity.
- Overall billing patterns suggest standardized pricing across patient visits.

## Cost drivers by medical condition
Billing totals are closely associated with patient volume rather than large differences in per-patient costs.
- Diabetes has the highest total billing $238.5M.  
- Obesity and arthritis follow closely in total cost.  
- Average billing per patient is similar ~$25K–$25.8K across conditions.  
- Differences in total cost are driven primarily by volume.

## Admission type analysis
Billing patterns are similar across admission types, with volume driving total cost differences.
- Elective admissions account for the highest total billing ~$477.6M.
- Urgent and emergency admissions follow closely.
- Average billing per admission is nearly identical across all admission types.
- Admission severity does not appear to significantly impact per-patient cost.

## Length of stay 
Length of stay varies only slightly by admission type.
- Emergency admissions have the longest average stay ~15.6 days.
- Elective admissions average ~15.5 days.
- Urgent admissions average ~15.4 days.
- Overall differences in length of stay are minimal.

## Length of stay by medical condition (min 1000 patients)
Hospital stay duration is consistent across medical conditions.
- Asthma patients have the longest average length of stay ~15.7 days.
- Arthritis and cancer follow closely.
- Diabetes patients have the shortest average stay ~15.4 days.
- Length of stay appears standardized across conditions.

## Insurance provider distribution
Insurance coverage shows balanced patient distribution and similar billing outcomes.
- Cigna covers the largest number of patients (11,249).
- Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross, and Aetna have similar patient counts.
- Total billing is evenly distributed across providers.
- Average billing per patient varies minimally across insurers.
